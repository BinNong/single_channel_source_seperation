CRITICAL FINDING (binning artifact in the published metric)
  evaluate.py stores known_snr = repeat(snr_kk, 2) while the known-pool
  score/label arrays are stacked tile-wise ([slot-1 for all samples,
  then slot-2]).  ensemble_analysis bins by the stored array, so the
  known entries of a "per-SNR bin" actually come from OTHER true-SNR
  bins (verified above: label multisets match only under tile binning).
  The unknown pool is binned exactly.  Each published bin therefore
  compares known vs unknown mixtures at DIFFERENT true SNRs, and since
  all OOD score scales drift with SNR, the apparent "energy wins at
  low SNR / prototype wins at high SNR" complementarity is produced by
  the SNR mismatch, not by OOD separability.  Under corrected, truly
  SNR-matched bins the GT-routed AUROC drops to the single-scorer
  level (~0.50): the routing gain does not survive fair binning, with
  GT or with estimated SNR.  On the paper's own (artifact-laden)
  metric, a real blind estimator reproduces the headline (see A1 rows
  and equivalent-sigma below), but that metric measures SNR-mismatch
  separability, not per-SNR OOD detection.
