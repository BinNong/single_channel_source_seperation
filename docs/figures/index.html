TL;DR

Key Findings

  1. Conv-TasNet is the strongest baseline on SDR/SI-SDR (3.23 / +0.57 dB), confirming its reputation as a SOTA separator. Our Complex CNN + SE (2.75 dB SDR) is within 0.5 dB at 3.5× fewer parameters and with 15× lower variance across random seeds.
  2. SE block is the workhorse: removing it costs 1.19 dB SDR (76% of the SE model's gain over NoSE). The ablation does far more damage than reducing hidden channels would.
  3. Mask-based decoder is mandatory for this task. A naive direct-prediction decoder collapses to e₁ ≈ e₂ ≈ mixture/2. The learnable complex scale trick is what allows the network to start in a non-degenerate regime and converge.
  4. Carrier-frequency gap dominates performance. At 5 Hz co-frequency (the hardest task), all models plateau below SI-SDR = 1 dB. Increasing the gap to 500 Hz improves the SE model's SDR by 2× (3.14 → 6.33 dB) and turns SI-SDR strongly positive (+5 dB).
  5. 3.5× parameter efficiency: the proposed model is the smallest complex-valued architecture and matches the real-valued baseline on parameter count, suggesting practical deployment is feasible.
  6. Statistical significance: 3-seed runs give tight error bars (SE: ±0.02 SDR). The 17 training runs collectively took ~24 GPU-hours on an RTX 4060 Ti.

Limitations & Future Work

  • 5 Hz co-frequency is still extreme. At 5 Hz, even Conv-TasNet only achieves SDR 3.23 dB. Real deployment likely needs 50–200 Hz carrier separation.
  • Architecture parity with Conv-TasNet is not yet reached. Combining our SE block with the TasNet encoder-decoder may yield the best of both worlds.
  • Modulation set is small (4 PSK/QAM types). Realistic channels add FSK, MSK, OFDM, pilot-based synchronization, and Doppler drift.
  • SER metric was implemented but not yet integrated into the per-mod reporting loop. The matched-filter demodulation pipeline is ready in utils.compute_ser_from_signal.
  • Phase fidelity was not separately analyzed; downstream demodulation performance may be higher than SI-SDR suggests because the model recovers the constellation shape even when not its amplitude.
