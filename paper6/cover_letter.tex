\noindent Dear Editor,

\vspace{0.4em}

We are pleased to submit the above manuscript for consideration in
\emph{IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing}. The paper addresses a gap
in the single-channel blind source separation (SC-BSS) literature: the
field trains and ranks separators by waveform fidelity (SI-SDR/SDR), but a
receiver's deliverable is bits, and we show that for co-frequency
communication mixtures the two decouple \emph{structurally}. We give the
decoupling a theory backbone, locate its mechanism experimentally, and
derive the receiver structure the analysis points to. The work combines
signal-processing theory (detection error floors, identifiability,
Fisher analysis) with algorithm design and statistically controlled
evaluation, and we believe it fits the Transactions' scope in signal
processing theory, methods, and communications applications.

\paragraph{Key contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Theory with simulation validation.} For the symbol-rate
two-source mixture model we prove a joint-MAP union bound governed by the
sum-constellation minimum distance $d_{\min}(\varphi)$ and an \emph{exact}
separate-detection error floor under a discrete interferer (e.g.\ the
$1/2$ high-SNR floor for QPSK$+$QPSK at $0$\,dB SIR); a finite-symmetry
identifiability proposition showing that only the effective per-source
complex coefficients are recoverable from the population mixture law, up
to the constellations' rotational group, and only through the joint
discrete structure; and an exact two-tone frequency-CRB inflation
analysis (median $1.7\times$ under the benchmark's offset law) contrasted
with a communication-waveform Fisher analysis showing no two-source
coupling once symbols are known. Each statement is accompanied by a
Monte-Carlo or measurement validation, including a direct
estimator-versus-CRB experiment at $K{=}2$.
\item \textbf{Architecture diagnosis.} A controlled experiment ladder
(E3--E4) shows that per-slot blind synchronisation is captured by the
residual interferer's spectral line (SNR-flat $+8$--$11$ SER points,
reproduced cross-architecture with a $26\times$ larger published
separator), that slot-aided detection loses to mixture-level detection
because mask separation distorts the linear observation model, and that
the separate-detection floor is a property of the linear-residual regime
rather than of separation as such.
\item \textbf{A training-free joint receiver.} The ISI-aware ECM joint
synchroniser-detector on the raw $K{=}2$ mixture --- no oracle carriers,
phases, or symbols during inference --- ties the strongest waveform route
at the paper's separator quality (pooled SER $0.531$ vs.\ $0.534$), wins
at matched oracle frequencies against even the stronger CNSE-based route
($0.411$ vs.\ $0.444$), and leads every waveform route under genie-free
differential scoring ($0.372$ vs.\ $0.438$). A learning-free blind
carrier synchroniser matches an oracle receiver within $0.5$ SER points
for BPSK/QPSK bursts at $K{=}1$.
\item \textbf{Honest envelope.} The operating boundaries are measured,
not assumed: blind modulation-pair selection fails at the classifier, the
tested $K{=}3$ acquisition strategy fails by phantom-pair lock, and
frequency-separation, timing, and amplitude-imbalance robustness are
swept explicitly.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Statistical standard.}
The unit of inference is the mixture burst. Headline contrasts are paired
per-burst (700 paired bursts on the reference grid; 3500 across five
independent test grids) with paired-bootstrap confidence intervals and
Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, and ties are claimed only when the paired
interval contains zero. A demodulation-aware loss-level control and a
strong published SC-BSS baseline (CNSE) are included as controls.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
All code --- the synthetic generator, the baseline separator and its
training script, the receiver modules, and every evaluation script behind
the tables --- is publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/BinNong/single_channel_source_seperation/tree/main/paper6_sync_jd};
the benchmark is fully synthetic and reproducible from the stated seeds
(deterministic test seed 99999), and the exact command behind every
number is recorded in the repository's experiment log.

\paragraph{Related submissions.}
Companion manuscripts on closed-set and open-world separator design are
under review elsewhere; they share no results with the present paper,
which is fully self-contained (models, generator, and baselines are
re-derived or vendored, and only published work is cited).
