\begin{abstract}
Evaluating out-of-distribution (OOD) detection for communication
signals is routinely conditioned on the operating SNR\@. Because OOD
score distributions drift with noise level, we show this conditioning
is fragile to label--score misalignment: storing per-source SNR labels
interleaved (\texttt{np.repeat}) while the scores they annotate are
stacked slot-wise (\texttt{np.tile}) manufactures a compelling,
spurious phenomenon in which two OOD scorer families appear
complementary across SNR and a training-free SNR-routed ensemble
appears to lift AUROC from 0.50 to $0.625 \pm 0.031$, with apparent
multi-seed significance, robustness, and graceful degradation. All of
it is an artifact of the misalignment. We formalize the failure as an
indexing-permutation confound and show it is general: \emph{any}
label--SNR decorrelation manufactures spurious per-SNR structure
(routed weighted AUROC up to $0.77$). We explain why the artifact
survives exactly the checks practitioners trust, and report the
corrected study under a deployment-faithful protocol: per-source
detection of unknown modulations from separation embeddings is at
chance across six scorers, five seeds, two backbones, and all tested
conditions (routed $0.498 \pm 0.020$). Attribution experiments show
the separator treats known and unknown sources alike and that a
partial embedding margin present for interference-free sources
(Mahalanobis AUROC $0.65$) does not survive separation. An evaluation
checklist is provided.
\end{abstract}

Blind source separation, open-set recognition, out-of-distribution

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\IEEEPARstart{S}{ingle-channel} blind source separation (SC-BSS) of
co-frequency communication signals is increasingly paired with
per-source modulation identification~\cite{hou2022single,guo2024single},
and a deployed receiver must additionally \emph{reject} sources whose
modulation it has never seen---an open-set recognition
problem~\cite{scheirer2013open,li2023expert} for which post-hoc OOD
scorers of learned embeddings and logits
\cite{hendrycks2017msp,liang2018odin,liu2020energy,lee2018mahalanobis,du2022vos}
are the standard toolkit. Because OOD score distributions drift with
noise, communication-signal evaluation is routinely conditioned on the
operating SNR---per-SNR-bin profiles and weighted
averages~\cite{proakis2008digital,oshea2017intro,li2023expert}.

This letter shows that the conditioning step itself is a single point
of failure. A one-line labeling-convention mismatch misaligns every
known-pool score with the wrong SNR bin, and because score scales drift
with SNR, each ``per-SNR bin'' then compares known and unknown pools at
\emph{different} true SNRs: the bins measure SNR separability, not OOD
separability. The artifact is dangerous precisely because it is
\emph{systematic}: it survives multi-seed averaging, significance
testing, and robustness batteries---the safeguards practitioners trust
\cite{kapoor2023leakage,musgrave2020reality}. We contribute:
(i)~a formalization of the failure as an indexing-permutation confound
and a synthetic-permutation study showing it is \emph{general}---any
label--SNR decorrelation, not only this bug, manufactures spurious
per-SNR gains (routed weighted AUROC up to $0.765$)---together with an
explanation of why exactly the trusted checks fail to catch it;
(ii)~a deployment-faithful evaluation protocol (verified alignment,
strict train/reference/test separation, unknown-free calibration,
operating-point metrics); and (iii)~the corrected study on an open-set
SC-BSS benchmark: a systematic \emph{negative} result with attribution,
covering six scorers, five seeds, two backbones, channel and capacity
interventions, leave-one-modulation-out (LOMO) splits, per-class
near/far-OOD breakdowns, a calibrated single-threshold operating point,
and an interference-free oracle probe.

\section{Open-Set SC-BSS Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\emph{Signal model and SNR definition.} We study the two-source
co-frequency mixture $y(t) = \alpha_1 s_1(t) + \alpha_2 s_2(t) + n(t)$
at complex baseband. Each $s_i$ is a unit-power synthetic waveform
(\SI{16}{kHz} sampling, 256 symbols at 16 samples/symbol,
root-raised-cosine pulse shaping with roll-off 0.35, 3-tap complex
Gaussian multipath normalized to unit gain, carriers
\SI{2000}{Hz}/\SI{2005}{Hz} with $\pm$\SI{5}{Hz} uniform jitter,
$T = 4096$ samples~\cite{proakis2008digital}), mixed with
$\alpha_1 = \alpha$, $\alpha_2 = 1 - \alpha$, $\alpha \sim U(0.4,
0.6)$, plus AWGN\@. Throughout, SNR denotes the \emph{mixture} SNR
$\snr_{\text{mix}} = 10\log_{10}\bigl(\mathbb{E}|\alpha_1 s_1 +
\alpha_2 s_2|^2 / \mathbb{E}|n|^2\bigr)$---the quantity a blind
receiver can estimate from $y$ alone. Since the sources are
unit-power, the per-source SNR of source $i$ is
$\snr_i = \snr_{\text{mix}} + 10\log_{10}\!\bigl(\alpha_i^2 /
(\alpha_1^2 + \alpha_2^2)\bigr) \approx \snr_{\text{mix}} -
\SI{3}{dB}$ under near-equal mixing, and the SIR
$20\log_{10}(\alpha_1/\alpha_2)$ sweeps $[-3.5, 3.5]$ dB with the
$\alpha$ draw. The per-source label stored by the evaluation pipeline
is this shared $\snr_{\text{mix}}$.

\emph{Modulations, protocols, pools.} Modulations split into known
$\mathcal{K} = \{$BPSK, QPSK, 8PSK, 16QAM$\}$ (training only) and
held-out $\mathcal{U} = \{$64QAM, $\pi$/4-DQPSK, MSK, OFDM-QPSK$\}$;
test protocols are kk/ku/uu by the number of unknown sources
(deterministic seed 99999; 192 kk, 192 ku, and 96 uu mixtures per bin
of the grid $\{-10, -5, \dots, 20\}$ dB). The \emph{known pool} holds
both slots of the kk mixtures and the \emph{unknown pool} the OOD slot
of each ku mixture plus both slots of uu (2688 sources each; 384 per
SNR bin). The separator is a lightweight complex-valued CNN with
complex squeeze-and-excitation (C-SE) attention ($\sim$69K parameters)
plus a shared per-source modulation head (64-d embedding $z$, 4 logits
$f$), trained with permutation-invariant SI-SDR + cross-entropy
\cite{kolbaek2017pit,leroux2019sdr} on known modulations only.
Closed-set reference points (kk): SI-SDR $-0.89$ dB pooled, rising
from $-4.9$ dB at \SI{-10}{dB} to $+0.6$ dB at \SI{20}{dB};
modulation accuracy $0.407$---an interference-limited regime
(SIR~$\approx 0$~dB) in which separation is intrinsically hard; this
regime is the stress test under which per-source OOD detection would
have to operate.

\emph{Scorers and leakage control.} Six post-hoc OOD scorers (larger =
more OOD) are applied per source: Energy $-\log\sum_k
e^{f_k}$~\cite{liu2020energy}, MSP~\cite{hendrycks2017msp},
ODIN~\cite{liang2018odin}, Mahalanobis~\cite{lee2018mahalanobis},
nearest-prototype distance, and a VOS-inspired (training-free,
post-hoc) virtual-outlier distance~\cite{du2022vos}. All scorer
hyperparameters are literature defaults fixed \emph{a priori}
(Energy/MSP temperature 1; ODIN $T{=}1000$, $\varepsilon{=}0.005$;
Mahalanobis shared covariance, shrinkage 0.1; VOS-inspired scale 2,
100 outliers/class); every fitted quantity (prototypes, covariance,
virtual outliers, thresholds) is estimated on a disjoint reference
pool (seed 88888, kk protocol), and \textbf{no unknown-class sample is
used for any fitting, selection, or calibration step}---the routing
boundary (\SI{0}{dB}) is fixed a priori, and the LOMO study selects
boundaries exclusively on a further pseudo-OOD validation split (seed
77777). The rejection threshold $\tau$ is the 95th percentile of
known-pool reference scores (5\% false-rejection target). The a-priori
\emph{SNR-routed} score uses Energy at $\snr \leq \SI{0}{dB}$ and
Prototype otherwise.

\section{The Pitfall}
\label{sec:pitfall}

\emph{Mechanism and formalization.} Index the test mixtures
$m = 1, \dots, N$ in SNR-block order. The evaluation stores per-source
scores $q_j$ stacked \emph{tile-wise} and per-source SNR labels
$\ell_j$ written \emph{interleaved} (\texttt{np.repeat}), so score $j$
belongs to mixture $\sigma_q(j) = ((j-1) \bmod N) + 1$ but is paired
with the label of mixture $\sigma_\ell(j) = \lceil j/2 \rceil$: an
indexing-permutation mismatch $\sigma_q \neq \sigma_\ell$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:mechanism}). Let $c(m)$ be the true SNR of mixture $m$
and suppose the score distribution drifts with SNR,
$p(s \mid c) \neq p(s \mid c')$. Binning by $\ell$ assigns to stored
bin $b$ scores whose \emph{true} SNRs are
$\{c(\sigma_q(j)) : c(\sigma_\ell(j)) = b\} \neq \{b\}$ wherever the
permutations disagree; the unknown pool, whose labels were exact, is
unaffected. Each per-bin AUROC then estimates
$P(s_u > s_k \mid c_u = b,\ c_k \in C_b)$ with $C_b \neq \{b\}$: it
measures the score's \emph{SNR separability}, not OOD separability,
with a magnitude set by the deterministic label--truth SNR gap (up to
\SI{15}{dB} here). A one-line audit exposes the corruption: binning the
known pool's stored \emph{modulation} labels by the stored SNR labels
gives per-bin class counts $114/96/100/74$, impossible for a protocol
contributing exactly $96$ mixtures per known modulation per true bin;
the corrected (tile) labels recover $96/96/96/96$.

\emph{Generality: any label--SNR decorrelation manufactures the
phenomenon.} The repeat-vs-tile bug is one member of a class. Re-binning
the \emph{corrected} scores with synthetic label permutations $\pi$
over the known pool (5-seed mean weighted AUROC of the a-priori routed
score): $\pi_{\text{identity}} = 0.498$; $\pi_{\text{repeat}}$ (the
original bug) $= 0.625$; $\pi_{\text{reverse}} = 0.765$; ten uniform
\emph{random} permutations $= 0.641 \pm 0.005$; block-structure
preserving shifts are exact no-ops ($0.498$). Per-bin AUROCs reach
$0.85$--$0.87$ under $\pi_{\text{reverse}}$. Two confounds combine:
mismatched known bins separate by their true-SNR composition (under
$\pi_{\text{random}}$ each bin approximates ``full known pool vs
unknown at $b$'', exposing monotone SNR gradients---Energy's mismatched
AUROC decreases with $b$ while Prototype's increases), and the fixed
a-priori routing rule harvests both gradients by construction. Notably,
single-scorer weighted averages stay near $0.48$--$0.51$ under every
$\pi$: the average can look innocent while the per-bin structure is
entirely spurious---which is exactly how the artifact presented as
\emph{complementarity} rather than as a global lift. Label--index
alignment is therefore not an implementation detail but a
confound-control condition for conditioned evaluation.

\emph{The manufactured phenomenon.} Under the buggy labels the
benchmark presented a perfect complementarity (Prototype $0.84$ AUROC
at \SI{10}{dB}, collapsing below \SI{0}{dB}; Energy $0.74$ at
\SI{-5}{dB}, falling to $0.22$ at \SI{10}{dB}), and routing on it
appeared to lift the pair-weighted AUROC from $0.50$ to $0.625 \pm
0.031$ (five seeds) against a post-hoc oracle of $0.681$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:money}a). It also produced a complete supporting
narrative: ``graceful degradation'' under simulated SNR-estimation
error ($\sigma = 1/3/6$ dB $\to$ $0.597/0.593/0.576$), ``robustness''
to SIR/timing/carrier perturbations ($0.58$--$0.66$), and
``consistent'' ablations (center loss $0.568$, carrier gaps
$0.615$--$0.633$, embedding dims $0.588$--$0.631$).

\emph{Why it survives the standard checks.} The misalignment is a
deterministic function of the data layout, identical for every seed
and condition: multi-seed averaging \emph{confirms} it ($\pm 0.031$),
and significance tests are significant precisely because the
SNR-mismatch signal is real---what is wrong is the bins'
interpretation. Robustness perturbations act on the signals, not the
label geometry, so each pass reads as independent evidence; the
$\sigma$-sweep's graceful decay is exactly what the artifact predicts,
since noising the SNR merely blurs the mismatch signal. And the
pattern is mechanistically narratable (``confidence inverts at high
SNR''), which is how it survives internal scrutiny. The discriminating
check is the one nobody thinks to run: verifying that the
\emph{labels} are aligned.

\emph{How it was caught.} Replacing the oracle SNR in the router with
a real blind SNR estimator (median-eigenvalue subspace estimator,
validated at \SI{0.51}{dB} overall RMSE; the classical M2M4 is
inadequate for this signal class~\cite{pauluzzi2000snr}) required
pairing waveform-derived estimates with stored scores sample by
sample. The alignment verification written for that
purpose---regenerating the deterministic test set and checking stored
arrays against the data order---failed immediately and exposed the
repeat-vs-tile mismatch.

\section{Corrected Protocol and Results}
\label{sec:corrected}

\emph{Protocol.} (i) \textbf{Verified label alignment}: every dumped
label array is checked against regenerated data (sequence equality,
per-bin counts, per-bin label multisets). (ii) \textbf{Strict
separation}: training, the reference pool (seed 88888), and the test
set (seed 99999) never mix; all fitted OOD quantities come from the
reference pool. (iii) \textbf{Unknown-free calibration}: $\tau$ is the
95th percentile of known-pool reference scores; no unknown-class data
is used for any fitted quantity, and the routing boundary is fixed a
priori. (iv) \textbf{Operating-point metrics}---Det@$\tau$, FPR95,
OSCR~\cite{dhamija2018oscr}, joint known/unknown accuracy---alongside
per-SNR and pooled AUROC\@. Seed standard deviations describe
training-run variability; we additionally report 95\% bootstrap
confidence intervals over test samples---the two uncertainties are
distinct.

\emph{Corrected results.} Under verified labels every scorer is flat
at chance at every SNR (Fig.~\ref{fig:money}b; Table~\ref{tab:master}):
the routed ensemble reaches $0.498 \pm 0.020$, and the post-hoc oracle
over all six scorers $0.549 \pm 0.032$, marginally above chance by
per-bin max-selection alone. MSP sits consistently \emph{below} chance
($0.43$)---the known-class head is confidently wrong on unknown
modulations; Mahalanobis sits marginally above ($0.520 \pm 0.050$ at
seed level, though its concatenated-sample CI excludes
$0.5$), driven by far-OOD classes and high-SNR bins (below). Nothing
survives perturbation because nothing was there: the
SNR-estimator-noise sweep is flat ($0.501$--$0.502$), SIR/timing/CFO
conditions give $0.487$--$0.494$, carrier gaps
$10$--\SI{500}{Hz} give $0.496$--$0.503$, center loss $0.487$,
embedding dims $16$--$128$ give $0.477$--$0.508$. LOMO splits (routing
boundary selected exclusively on pseudo-OOD validation data) stay at
$0.482$--$0.509$, and the selected boundary is unstable (9/12 runs pick
\SI{0}{dB}; 3/12 find no crossover and fall back to all-Prototype)---
there is no crossover to find. A second backbone without the C-SE
blocks (weaker closed-set model) behaves identically: routed $0.508
\pm 0.011$, no complementarity.

\emph{Operating point.} At the calibrated single threshold
(Table~\ref{tab:op}) the detector rejects $5.8\%$ of unknown sources
against a $5.2\%$ false-rejection rate---a near-useless trade that
FPR95 ($0.954$), OSCR ($0.196$) and joint accuracy ($0.163$) confirm.
Per SNR, Det@$\tau$ is \emph{exactly} $0$ at $\leq$\SI{0}{dB} and
rises to $0.14$ at \SI{20}{dB}, with the false-rejection rate tracking
it ($0 \to 0.139$): the global threshold tracks the known pool's
SNR-driven score drift and the unknown pool drifts identically. The
failure is distributional, not a threshold-choice artifact---no
operating point exists because the score distributions do not
separate.

\emph{Near vs far OOD.} Breaking the unknown pool down by class
(weighted-average AUROC, corrected): for embedding-distance scorers
the far-OOD families score weakly higher (Mahalanobis: MSK $0.556 \pm
0.041$, OFDM-QPSK $0.551 \pm 0.117$; Prototype: $0.500$/$0.527$) than
the near-OOD ones (64QAM $0.478$/$0.478$, $\pi$/4-DQPSK
$0.496$/$0.516$); for logit-based scorers the pattern
\emph{inverts}---MSP on OFDM-QPSK is $0.359 \pm 0.027$, i.e. the
multicarrier waveform looks \emph{more} in-distribution to the
known-class head than the known pool itself. Neither direction is
deployable; the near/far intuition does not transfer to post-hoc
scoring of separated sources.

\emph{Attribution.} Three controlled observations bound the
explanation. (1)~Separation quality is class-independent: SI-SDR of
known vs unknown sources is $-0.89$ vs $-1.00$ dB pooled (within
$0.21$ dB at every SNR bin)---the separator does not distort unknown
modulations more than known ones. (2)~A partial margin exists without
interference: applied to clean single-source inputs (a probe, not a
deployed configuration), embedding-geometry scorers reach Mahalanobis
AUROC $0.660 \pm 0.046$---rising from $0.52$ at \SI{-10}{dB} to $0.77$
at \SI{10}{dB}, and $0.82$ on the far-OOD OFDM waveform---while logit
scorers remain at or below chance ($0.42$--$0.44$) even there.
(3)~Under the two-source separated condition every scorer falls to
$\approx 0.5$ (Table~\ref{tab:master}). Together, the observed results
suggest that the separation and known-class objectives do not provide
a sufficiently exploitable known/unknown margin for post-hoc scorers
\emph{operating on separated sources}: a margin that partially exists
in the embedding does not survive the mixture/separation condition
(residual interference at SIR $\approx 0$ dB), and the logit pathway
never carries one. Scope: these conclusions hold for the evaluated
synthetic benchmark and protocol; dedicated open-set modulation
recognition~\cite{li2023expert} with unknown-aware training is a
separate, harder problem.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

A repeat-vs-tile labeling mismatch manufactured an entire
SNR-conditioned phenomenon that survived multi-seed averaging,
significance intuition, and every robustness check; a synthetic
permutation study shows the failure is a general indexing confound,
not a one-off bug. Corrected, and under a deployment-faithful
protocol, post-hoc OOD detection of unknown modulations from
separation embeddings remains at chance on the evaluated synthetic
SC-BSS benchmark. For conditioned OOD evaluation we recommend:
(1)~\emph{align labels against regenerated data} before trusting any
conditioned metric (sequence, per-bin counts, label multisets)---no
averaging or robustness battery substitutes for this check;
(2)~\emph{multi-seed averaging is not a pipeline check}---it averages
random error and confirms systematic error; (3)~\emph{separate
train/reference/test strictly} and calibrate operating points without
unknown-class data, reporting Det@$\tau$ alongside AUROC;
(4)~\emph{treat per-bin oracle bounds as bounds}, never as deployable
headlines. Code, generators, and both the corrected and archived
(artifact) dumps are released at
\url{https://github.com/BinNong/single_channel_source_seperation/tree/main/paper3_open_set}
(pinned release tag \texttt{paper3-spl}).
